\medskip
{\small\textbf{What this block says on the development data}}
\begin{itemize}\small
\item Firms that start exporting show no clear change in value added per GJ of energy: +9\% (95\% interval $-$8\% to +28\%), and
  +4\% to +10\% under the previous paper's four methods. Starts before and after Paris do not differ clearly.
\item The small, unclear rise in exporters' value added per GJ comes from value added growing (+19\%, clear), not from
  using less energy (energy use +9\%, not clear).
\item Firms that start importing show a clear rise, +29\% (+14\% to +46\%), in every version and under every method (+29\% to
  +36\%); but under one method's check over all earlier years they were already changing before the start.
\item Getting a foreign owner (10\%+ of capital) shows no clear change: +4\% ($-$12\% to +22\%); 0\% to +13\% by method.
\item Of the previous paper's six measures, only value added per GJ of bought electricity is clearly up after the export start
  (+27\%), and not against the average of the five years before the start (+17\%, not clear).
\item Three recording problems are repaired: import starts dated 2017, all 2020 foreign-ownership answers, and doubtful
  energy records in 2017 and 2019--2022. Firm links are fictional: every page tests the method; none is a finding.
\end{itemize}

years before, exports again the year after) leaves only 29 starters. In the table, 459 import starts fall in 2017 and 854

\expart{How it helps Deasy's request}{``Stagged did'' needs a start year for every firm: this page gives it, fully. For ``Mirip

\expart{What it is}{Value added per GJ of energy (fuels burned plus bought electricity) for 742 export starters, as \% change since
their year before the start, minus the change in the same years among 2,832 never-exporters. Each firm is compared with itself and

\expart{How to read it}{Black dot = one year since the start; hollow dot = 6 or more years before or after, grouped; gray diamond = the year before the start

Dots left of the dashed line are the before-start check: they should sit near 0.}
\expart{What it shows on this data}{Average of years 0 to +5: +9\% (95\% interval $-$8\% to +28\%), no clear change. Only year +5
is clearly above 0 (+45\%), and it rests on 169 starters from before 2016 and 19 from 2017. Before the start, all starters show no
clear difference (p = 0.245; below 0.05 would be clear), but starters seen not exporting the year just before do (p $<$ 0.001),

\expart{How it helps Deasy's request}{It answers ``Intertionalized and energy efficiency'' for exporting, with the ``Stagged did''
