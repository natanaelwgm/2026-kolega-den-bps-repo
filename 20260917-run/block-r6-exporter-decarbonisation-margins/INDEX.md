## Details kept out of the figures

**01 — Carbon productivity after an export start, and its parts.**
- Sample and model are R2's, reproduced exactly: the file stops if R2-02/03/04/05/08/09's tables differ. 742 starters and 2,832 never-exporters in the estimate, 20,070 firm-years; firm and year fixed effects; standard errors clustered by firm.
- Value added per tonne of CO2: +10% after the start (95% interval −6% to +29%), not clearly different from 0. Value added per GJ: +9%, not clear either.
- The parts: value added +19% (clear); energy used +9% (not clear, and it already differed before the start, p = 0.027); CO2 per GJ −1% (not clear); output +14% (clear).
- In logs the rows add up exactly, marker by marker (asserted in all 7 versions): (1) = (3) − (4) − (5) and (2) = (3) − (4).
- The CSV (623 rows) holds every year-by-year coefficient for 7 outcomes × 7 versions:
  - main;
  - without doubtful energy records (hollow gray; moves no answer);
  - without 2017 starters;
  - exact starters (output loses clarity; before-start check fails for value added per CO2, per GJ and energy, p < 0.001);
  - without 2018–19;
  - energy-weighted: **unstable, not used** — its weights act like about 19 starters and 2 never-exporters;
  - import starts (R2-08's repairs, years −4 to +4): value added per tonne of CO2 +24% and energy −13%, both clear; value added +12%, but its before-start check fails (p < 0.001).
- Companion tables: every plotted point (1 page) and the 12 regressions behind the dots (2 pages, coefficient over standard error, no stars).
- CO2 = energy × standard rates, so CO2 per GJ moves only with the fuel and electricity mix. Value added and output are nominal (no price index in the file).
- Shows timing, not which part caused what. Firm links are fictional: this tests the method, not a finding. Add-ons: reghdfe, ftools, require.

**02 — Fuels burned vs bought electricity after an export start.**
- Same sample block and model as R6-01, copied unchanged (block checked byte-identical). Energy used on all usable rows reproduces R6-01 row (4) in 4 versions (52 values; the file stops otherwise).
- Drawn: firm-years with both fuel and bought electricity above 0 (17,088; 703 starters, 2,491 never-exporters), so both panels follow the same firms.
- Average of years 0 to +5: fuels burned +17% (95% interval −4% to +42%), bought electricity −3% (−17% to +14%); neither clear. Before the start neither differed clearly (p = 0.292, 0.215). Value added on the same rows +20% (clear), energy used +14% (not clear).
- Single years whose interval excludes 0: fuels +2 (+38%, clear in 3 of 5 versions); bought electricity −3 (−18%, 4 of 5) and the start year (+30%, 5 of 5). Of 24 dots about 1 would be clear by chance, so the averages are the answer. In (b) the year before the start and the start year both sit above the other years, so bought electricity rises around the start and then returns to its earlier level.
- Five versions (CSV): all; without doubtful energy records; without 2017 starters; without 2017 rows (coal recorded for far more firms that year); only starters seen not exporting the year before. Fuels burned runs +13% to +31% and is clear only in the last (+31%, 95% interval +4% to +65%, 479 starters, before-start p = 0.306); bought electricity runs −6% to −1% and is never clear.
- Each on its own rows: fuels +19% (18,001 firm-years), bought electricity −5% (19,146); neither clear.
- Firms that use none of one are counted, not hidden: 2,168 usable firm-years burn no fuel, 1,011 buy no electricity, 345 more record no energy at all. After the start the share burning no fuel moves +0.6 points and buying none +1.1 points (both unclear; 20,362 firm-years).
- CO2 from bought electricity moves exactly with bought electricity (one national rate a year, removed by the year effects; asserted at every point), so it is not drawn. CO2 from fuels = fuels + CO2 per GJ of fuels (−0.4%, not clear) = +16%.
- Bought electricity excludes power the firm makes itself (its fuel is already in fuels burned; own generation is R6-04).
- Companion tables: every plotted point (26 rows, 1 page) and the 10 regressions (2 pages: years, then model rows with fixed effects, weights and clustering; coefficient over standard error, no stars).
- Shows timing, not cause. Firm links are fictional: this tests the method, not a finding. Add-ons: reghdfe, ftools, require.

**03 — Energy mix and CO2 per GJ after an export start.**
- Same starters, never-exporters and model as R6-01 (shared sample block copied exactly; the CO2-per-GJ row reproduces R6-01's row (5), the file stops otherwise). 742 starters and 2,832 never-exporters; 20,070 firm-years, 18,001 of them with some fuel (fuel shares use only those).
- Main (all fuels in the file): oil products' share of fuel −4.3 points (95% interval −7.5 to −1.1) and gas's share +2.8 (0.3 to 5.3) are clearly different from 0; bought electricity's share of energy −1.3, electricity's share of CO2 −1.9, coal +1.5, CO2 per GJ −1.3% and CO2 per GJ of fuels −0.3% are not.
- **Not a clear fuel switch.** Counting only the four fuels asked in every file (diesel, petrol, coal, LPG), oil is −1.9 (−5.0 to 1.2) and gas +0.7 (−1.6 to 2.9), both not clear: part of the shift comes from fuels added to or dropped from the questions (kerosene asked 2010–2015, city gas 2010–2019, other gas 2012–2014 and 2017–2019, coal briquettes 2012–2014 and 2017–2022). The reviewer's check puts the whole clear gas rise in city gas and other gas (+2.1 points); LPG +0.7 is not clear; gas's share 2 to 5 years before the start was already about as high (+0.5 against those years).
- Coal's share already differed before the start (p = 0.035; 0.018 with the four fuels). It is clear only for starters seen not exporting the year before (+3.5), where the before-start check fails (p < 0.001).
- 2017: coal is 37% of the average firm's fuel (4–7% before, 13–20% after); leaving every 2017 row out keeps oil −4.8 and gas +3.3. The share of firms burning some coal stays high 2017–2019 (36–38%), which the year effects absorb only where starters and never-exporters are alike.
- Each firm's own shares count equally; the file's summed fuel is never used (a few firms make coal up to 99.9% of it in a year). Natural gas, biomass and fuel oils (2020–2022 only) are outside the energy total, so gas is under-counted then.
- CSV (728 rows): 8 rows (the 7 drawn + "burns some coal") × 7 versions (main, four every-year fuels, without 2017 rows, without doubtful records, without 2017 starters, exact starters, without 2018–19) × every year since the start. Oil stays clear in 4 of the 5 other versions and gas in 2 of 5 (the exact-starter version fails the before-start check for oil). Companion tables: every plotted point (1 page) and the regressions (2 pages; Panels B and C averages only, year by year in the CSV).
- CO2 = GJ × standard rates, so CO2 per GJ moves only with the mix. Shows timing, not cause; firm links are fictional: this tests the method, not a finding. Add-ons: reghdfe, ftools, require.

**04 — Own electricity after an export start.**
- Same sample block and model as R6-01, copied unchanged (block checked byte-identical). Only rows where own electricity is recorded; "not recorded" is never read as "makes none".
- 2014 and 2015 are left out of the main version: own electricity is recorded there for 62% of starters' rows but 46–48% of never-exporters' rows (all rows 50–52%), and 39–41% of recorded rows make some against 20% in 2013. Rule computed from the data: a year is used only if both groups reach 75% (as in R1-05, R1-07).
- (a) Firms making some: +8.1 points after the start (average of years 0 to +5; 95% interval +3.7 to +12.4), clear; without doubtful energy records +5.2 (clear). With 2014 and 2015 counted: +5.7 (clear) and +3.0 without doubtful records (not clear). Both pairs are on the chart. The year before the start 27% of starters made some; never-exporters 24%. 16,589 firm-years; 722 starters, 2,640 never-exporters.
- Before the start: joint test p = 0.100, but the single year −5 is clear (+8.9 points, clear in 5 of 7 versions), so starters already made more of their own electricity well before starting.
- Seven versions (CSV): (a) runs +3.0 to +12.3 points, clear in 6 of 7 (not with 2014–15 counted and doubtful records out). The highest, exact starters (seen not exporting the year before), fails the before-start check (p = 0.006), so it is not the answer.
- (b) Own share of electricity used: +1.7 points (0.0 to +3.4), not clear; +0.5 to +2.0 across versions, clear only for exact starters (whose before-start check fails, p = 0.038). Starters made 7% of their electricity the year before, never-exporters 5%.
- (c) Amount made, among firms making some in at least two years (354 starters, 3,495 firm-years): +27% (−26% to +117%), not clear; +6% to +69% across versions, never clear. Without doubtful records only 240 starters remain, so the gray triangles swing widely (up to +276% in one year).
- No single year is clear in (b) or (c); in (a) seven of 12 are (−5 and every year from +1). Of 36 dots about 2 would be clear by chance.
- Table only: electricity used (bought + own) +5% (−11% to +24%), not clear — a separate measure, not energy used.
- Makers' own electricity per unit bought drops from the 2017 file on (middle firm 0.16–0.27 before, 0.01–0.08 after): likely a recording change; year effects remove the part all firms share.
- Companion tables: every plotted point (75 rows, 3 pages) and the 8 regressions (2 pages; fixed-effects, weights and clustering rows; no stars).
- Cannot show why firms make their own electricity or which fuel feeds the generator. Shows timing, not cause. Firm links are fictional: this tests the method, not a finding. Add-ons: reghdfe, ftools, require.

**05 — CO2 after an export start under three sets of emission rates.**
- CO2 in the file is not measured: GJ of each fuel × one rate per fuel, plus bought kWh × a national grid rate. Three sets, all stored in the data file (which does not name their outside source): main (grid 0.60 kg CO2 per kWh in 2010 rising to 0.91 in 2014, then kept to 2022); grid rate fixed at 0.87 in every year; scenario 2 (0.87 and other fuel rates, −29% to +14% of main by fuel). Every rate is listed in the points-table footer.
- Same sample block and model as R6-01, copied unchanged (block checked byte-identical); under the main rates the rows reproduce R6-01 (208 values), R6-02 (65) and R6-03 (65) or the file stops. 742 starters, 2,832 never-exporters, 20,070 firm-years.
- Average of years 0 to +5, main / grid fixed / scenario 2: value added per tonne of CO2 +10.2 / +11.0 / +11.3%; CO2 emitted +7.6 / +6.9 / +6.6% (already differed before the start, p = 0.022); CO2 per GJ −1.3 / −2.0 / −2.2%; CO2 from fuels +18.8 / +18.8 / +17.6%; CO2 from bought electricity −5.1% in all three; electricity's share of CO2 −1.9 / −2.0 / −1.8 points. None clearly different from 0 under any set. Other rates move a % change by at most 1.2 percentage points.
- CO2 from bought electricity cannot change between sets, by design: its rate is the same for all firms in a year and the year fixed effects remove it (asserted at every point). So the grid-rate check moves the other rows only through each firm's electricity share.
- Five versions (CSV): the sets disagree on clear or not only for starters seen not exporting the year before, and there those starters already differed before the start: CO2 per GJ −4.0% clear under scenario 2 (main −2.8%; p = 0.041), electricity's share −3.6 points clear with the grid rate fixed (main −3.4; p = 0.017). CO2 from fuels for those starters is +31.8%, clear under all three sets (before-start p = 0.154).
- Single years: 17 of 72 have an interval that excludes 0 under the main rates (about 4 by chance); listed with their counts of 5 versions under the regression table. Other rates move 3 of the 72 between clear and not clear.
- Companion tables: every plotted point with every rate (18 rows, 1 page) and 3 panels × 8 regressions (2 pages; year by year for the main rates; fixed-effects, weights and clustering rows; no stars).
- Cannot show measured CO2 or a grid rate after 2014. Shows timing, not cause. Firm links are fictional: this tests the method, not a finding. Add-ons: reghdfe, ftools, require.

**06 — Five changes after an export start, each under five methods.**
- Same sample block as R6-01, copied unchanged (block checked byte-identical in 01–06). The one-regression rows reproduce R6-01 rows (3), (4), (5), (1), R6-03 row (a1) and R6-04 panel (a) in 3 versions each (every year, average, before-start p, rows) or the file stops. 742 starters (740 with a row from the start year on) and 2,832 never-exporters, 20,070 firm-years; own electricity 16,589 (2014–15 left out by the 75% recording rule, 1,767 firm-years not recorded).
- Average of years 0 to +5, starters minus never-exporters, one regression / Callaway–Sant'Anna / Sun–Abraham / Borusyak–Jaravel–Spiess / de Chaisemartin–D'Haultfœuille: value added +18.6 / +14.3 / +13.9 / +21.8 / +14.3%, clear in all five; energy used +9.0 / +5.7 / +3.4 / +16.8 / +5.7%, clear only under Borusyak–Jaravel–Spiess (95% interval +0.3% to +36%; its before-start p = 0.050); CO2 per GJ −1.3 / −2.3 / −1.9 / −0.3 / −2.3%, never clear; bought electricity's share −1.3 / −2.2 / −1.6 / −0.8 / −2.2 points, never clear; firms making own electricity +8.1 / +6.4 / +7.9 / +5.9 / +6.4 points, clear in all five. Value added per tonne of CO2 (table only) +4.6% to +12.2%, never clear; it equals value added − energy used − CO2 per GJ exactly in every method (asserted).
- Before the start, each method's own check (p below 0.05 = starters already differed): energy used fails under the one regression (0.027) and Callaway–Sant'Anna (0.002); value added (0.017) and CO2 per GJ (0.038) fail under Callaway–Sant'Anna. Callaway–Sant'Anna over all years before the start fails for 4 of 5 (not own electricity, 0.276). Starters seen not exporting the year before (table): fail for energy, CO2 per GJ, electricity share and own electricity.
- Start years left out: Callaway–Sant'Anna and de Chaisemartin–D'Haultfœuille by construction, Sun–Abraham before the call (no row the year before): 2017 starters (37; no 2016 file), and for own electricity 2015 starters too (135 in all). De Chaisemartin–D'Haultfœuille's before-start check for own electricity covers years −2 and −3 only (no starter has the rows year −4 needs); this placebo reduction is new code beyond R2-07.
- Callaway–Sant'Anna and de Chaisemartin–D'Haultfœuille give the same averages here (both compare each start year with never-exporters from the year before), as in R2-07.
- Single years: 20 of 60 clear in the one regression (15 after the start, 5 before), about 3 by chance; listed with their method counts in the points-table notes, not drawn.
- Companion tables: every plotted point (30 rows, 2 pages) and one regression page per change (6 pages; 7 columns: the five methods, one regression without doubtful energy records, exact starters; year by year, sample and model rows; no stars).
- Shows timing, not cause. Firm links are fictional: this tests the method, not a finding. Runs about 5 minutes. Add-ons (SSC, installed once; the file installs nothing): reghdfe, ftools, require; csdid, drdid; eventstudyinteract, avar; did_imputation; did_multiplegt_dyn, gtools. A missing method package gives an "unavailable" row and the file still finishes.
