# Governing claim and linked appendix architecture

The exercise can compare how three estimators remove observed input contributions from the same nominal development records; its fictional identities, nominal measurements, and numerical sensitivity prevent interpreting those residuals as verified real productivity.

1. **The question is whether output remains high after accounting for inputs.** Explain purpose, data, three main findings, and a labeled roadmap. A reader stopping here understands what the appendix establishes.
2. **A production residual is a conditional comparison, not output per worker.** Derive levels, logs, native residuals, unit transformations, centering and exponentiation. Distinguish persistent productivity from transitory and price components.
3. **Available stock histories support 2012 estimation and retrospective 2011 predictions.** Map every input field, source units, positivity, missingness, concept flags, capital timing, sector and calendar samples.
4. **LP uses materials to represent information behind input choices.** Explain economic inversion, conventional estimation, exact implemented stages, assumptions, mapped inputs, worked aggregate arithmetic, coefficient/residual/year/tail evidence and sensitivity.
5. **OP needs usable investment and a separate survival sample.** Explain all regular selection-corrected stages and exact adaptation, then show where real observed support fails. No invented OP coefficient, residual or histogram.
6. **ACF moves labor identification to intertemporal moments.** Explain functional dependence and all stages/instruments, then method-specific results, wide intervals, distributions, tails, yearly predictions and initialization dependence.
7. **Wooldridge combines equations with different instruments.** Distinguish general equation-specific proxy approximations from implemented shared nuisance coefficients, then all own-method evidence and sensitivities.
8. **Shared-record rankings can agree despite different coefficient estimates.** Compare intersections, correlations and bins; distinguish nominal levels and ranks.
9. **Resampling and alternative specifications expose distinct uncertainties.** Explain fixed-company-cluster bootstrap, conditioning, omitted generator uncertainty, outliers and declared sensitivity rather than claiming robustness.
10. **The findings justify a descriptive comparison, while economic interpretation remains conditional.** Synthesize measured agreement, unsupported OP and next evidential requirements through dynamic references to the earlier sections.

Each estimator is a parallel child argument: economic question → mathematical stages → data translation → calculation example → evidence → interpretation. No page quota or decorative repetition. All headings, equations, tables and figures use labels; the standalone wrapper supplies placement and numbering. Deep Stata aggregates add actual method/year/sector diagnostics, not page padding.

QC targets: first-minute clarity, headings-only story, sibling parity, parent-child support, complete exhibit interpretation, no naked hard-coded exhibit numbers, no 2011 re-estimation claim, no OP estimate, no global-optimum claim, distinction between LP/ACF cubic transitions and Wooldridge proxy-basis changes.
