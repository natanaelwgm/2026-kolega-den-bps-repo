# Nominal-development diagnostics across all supplied years

N08–N13 describe the frozen G0.5 nominal-development results under the owner's maintained capital assumption. They use the coefficients and bootstrap draws already saved by N01–N07. They never fit a new coefficient, resample a company, alter an input, or replace an earlier run receipt. The source manifest and output fields are declared in `../../deep-diagnostics-contract.json` before execution.

The primary `G1.do` takes repository, distinct external diagnostics-run directory, `diagnostics`, and read-only base-run arguments, then dispatches to `G1_deep.do`. It reads narrow S01/N01/N03/N04 files and existing coefficient/bootstrap CSVs. The twelve public outputs are aggregate CSVs. Source observations, fictional identifiers, and record-level working files remain outside the repository.

## Calendar coverage and denominators

- The twelve source years are 2010–2015 and 2017–2022. The annual grid retains every source year, including an explicit empty prediction obligation when beginning capital cannot be constructed. `source_year=0` denotes a pooled group, never a calendar year. Original source values and documentary verification flags remain unchanged.
- N08 describes each input's missing, zero, negative, positive and usable-log domains. We assume reported capital totals are year-end stocks in every supplied year. Beginning capital is the exact preceding-calendar-year reported stock on the same fictional company; no 2009 or 2016 value is invented. Positive reported additions are a separately assumed investment candidate and are never derived by subtracting stocks. `N_concept_fail` counts failures of these maintained assumptions' operational domain (for beginning capital, an absent preceding company-year). It does not count source-documentation failures. For ordinary output, labor and materials this concept count is not applicable. `N_log_usable` requires positivity and the maintained concept domain; it certifies neither real prices nor verified linkage. The pooled `production_year_supported` is missing because a pooled group is not a single year.
- N09 begins the sequential production funnel at original source rows in the named year and sector. `prediction_branch` returns to the method/sample's positive domain; it is not a continuation of the dynamic estimation restriction. OP selection has a separate exact-calendar synthetic risk-interval denominator grouped by endpoint year. Continuers and disappearances partition the complete-lag risk sample. Source-year zero pools those intervals and can count a fictional company more than once. `ALL` summarizes separate sector fits; it never combines sectors to pass a support threshold.
- N10 uses saved primary residuals and indices. Every prediction year uses the same sector-method-sample coefficients and the same mean raw residual from that cell's pooled dynamic fit records. `coefficient_year` and `normalizer_year` remain missing; `coefficient_year_min/max` describe the fitted range and `normalizer_scope=pooled_dynamic` states the reference sample. The valid production years are 2011–2015 and 2018–2022; dynamic fit endpoints are 2012–2015 and 2019–2022. `N_source`, `N_domain`, `N_predicted`, `N_in_fit` and `N_outside_fit` describe the whole named year/sector/method/sample; `N` describes the finite measure in the selected fit scope.
- The index is `exp(centered log residual)` with multiplier one. Its geometric mean is one within the corresponding pooled dynamic fit records. Its arithmetic mean need not be one, and it is not a physical productivity measure. The declared strict IQR fences, centered-log thresholds and index ratios identify tails without trimming any observation. Each histogram's share equals its bin count divided by its exact group size. Index bins are geometric and equally spaced in centered-log coordinates. Unoccupied bins remain gaps in the chart.
- N11 joins primary predictions by exact fictional company, same sector, method, sample and adjacent calendar years. The obligation grid contains all ten adjacent source-year pairs. The 2010→2011 and 2017→2018 prediction pairs are empty because their origin years lack beginning capital. Eight intervals can contain valid prediction pairs. No interval crosses 2016. `year_from=year_to=0` pools record-pair intervals, not unique companies; a company can contribute several adjacent-year changes. `N_from` and `N_to` count endpoint prediction sets across those intervals. `N_pairs` requires both predictions in the same sector; its difference from either endpoint count does not separately identify why the partner failed. `N_to_in_fit` and `N_to_outside_fit` partition matched destination rows. `mean_index_ratio` always means the mean of each matched pair's destination index divided by its origin index, including when repeated on raw or centered measure rows. It is not the exponential of mean log change.
- N12 reports a pooled observed row and all twelve annual obligations for every sector-method-sample with a primary prediction. It uses means of logged inputs, reproducing mean raw residual with fixed slopes. `exp_centered` is the exponential of the mean centered residual, while `mean_index` is the mean of the individual exponentials. Annual groups below five rows have no released observed means or derived arithmetic. Each fitted cell also has one separate source-year-zero illustrative row with designed logs (10, 3, 8), the actual saved slopes, and `normalizer_value`. Its observation count is missing because it is not a company example. The normalizer is fixed across all years and recorded with missing `normalizer_year` and scope `pooled_dynamic`.
- N13 compares all existing variants only with their own sector-method-sample primary. A lower objective is a numerical diagnostic and does not replace the selected primary. Bootstrap summaries use saved successful draws, retain failures in the requested-draw denominator, and never pool objectives across methods. Their uncertainty remains conditional on the fictional linkage and maintained nominal specification.

These comparisons describe modified observations linked by fictional company identities. They do not identify actual productivity growth, closures, shocks or causal effects. Exact numerical summaries and correlations are suppressed for groups below five rows; counts and fixed histogram categories remain visible. An empty method obligation is not evidence that its estimator is intrinsically invalid.

## Validation, charts and immutable receipts

It also checks the source calendar, exact preceding-year stock, preserved documentary flags, fixed pooled normalizer, full annual obligation grid, same-company/same-sector paired keys, suppression, and output schema. A successful receipt is an arithmetic and provenance result, not empirical acceptance. The analytical source remains Stata.

The chart builder consumes these aggregate CSVs only. It includes every supported method, including OP; uses actual Times New Roman; binds each numerical annotation to its source cell; and splits annual quartile, interval-comparison, and sensitivity charts into legible continuation figures. Annual panels show all available quartiles with bold medians. Histogram labels follow one rule: first, modal and last occupied bins. Legends describe every encoding. Figures never bridge the absent 2016 annual file.
