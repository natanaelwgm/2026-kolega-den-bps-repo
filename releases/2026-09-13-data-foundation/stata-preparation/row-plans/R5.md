## Question

Do factories exporting to destinations with stricter environmental policy show different environmental productivity over time?

Build a dated, product-applicable policy exposure before choosing a causal claim; foreign destination sorting and demand shocks need explicit treatment.

Unit: Verified establishment×destination×product×year source flows, aggregated to establishment-year policy exposure.

### Target and meaning

w_id = sum_{b=2010}^{2012} exports_idb / sum_{d,b=2010}^{2012} exports_idb; Z_it = sum_d w_id EPS_dt; q_it = alpha_i + lambda_t + beta Z_it + u_it

beta is the within-establishment association between a one-point change in the weighted destination policy index and the named log productivity outcome. Baseline destination weights stay fixed; this is not a causal policy effect.

Destination policies and economic conditions can move together. Baseline exporters are selected. The OECD index covers a subset of destinations and ends in 2020. The fully covered primary sample may differ from other exporters.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.

## Finite estimation family

Primary outcomes: ln(V/E_total); ln(V/C_total).

Secondary outcomes: ln(E_total); ln(C_total); ln(V); export share; destination concentration.

Methods:

- Primary plant/year fixed-effect descriptive association using fixed destination EPS exposure
- Sensitivity first differences over genuine adjacent annual pairs
- Sensitivity baseline log employment × year and certified baseline industry × year
- Leave-one-out sensitivities for the three largest baseline export destinations by total baseline value across the eligible sample, ties broken by ISO code
- Separately gated specific-policy causal design; no empirical execution under this plan

- Baseline shares 2010–2012; outcome years 2013,2014,2015,2017,2018,2019,2020
