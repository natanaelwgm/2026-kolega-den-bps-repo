## Question

Do exporters improve through lower energy use, cleaner fuels, or expanding production?

Reuse the export-entry design from R2 to separate arithmetic components of productivity changes from evidence about mechanisms.

Unit: Verified establishment-year on exactly the R2 export cohort, event clock, control set and declared outcome sample.

### Target and meaning

ATT_k[ln(V/E)] = ATT_k[ln(V)] - ATT_k[ln(E)]; ATT_k[ln(V/C)] = ATT_k[ln(V)] - ATT_k[ln(C)]

The identities hold for the same sample, linear estimator, covariates and aggregation weights. Components show where the ratio changes; they do not identify causal mediation.

Different outcome domains or estimator weights break coefficient identities. Purchased electricity and own generation must not be double counted. Annual grid factors may change carbon without a plant changing its behavior.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.
