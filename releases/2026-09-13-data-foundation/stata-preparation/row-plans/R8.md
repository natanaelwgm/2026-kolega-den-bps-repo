## Question

Does starting to export change the products a factory makes?

Use a verified product panel and the R2 export-entry design to distinguish genuine product restructuring from recoding and reporting changes.

Unit: Verified establishment×harmonized product×calendar-year source, then establishment-year product outcomes.

### Target and meaning

n_it = count{h: y_iht>0}; s_iht = y_iht/sum_h y_iht; HHI_it=sum_h s_iht^2; core_it=max_h s_iht

Counts measure observed product scope; HHI and core share measure output-value concentration. Entry and exit compare the same product definition across adjacent observed years.

Changing product codes or reporting thresholds can mimic entry/exit. Value shares reflect prices as well as quantities. Product adjustments following export entry do not alone identify a causal mediation channel.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.
