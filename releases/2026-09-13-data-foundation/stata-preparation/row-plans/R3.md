## Question

Which factories reduce energy use and emissions, and which import environmental goods?

Describe within-factory patterns without turning reverse causality, output expansion, or a noisy baseline into a claim about motivation.

Unit: Verified establishment-year and true adjacent calendar-year pair; firm-product-year for the environmental-goods branch.

### Target and meaning

r^E_it = ln(E_i,t-1)-ln(E_it); r^C_it = ln(C_i,t-1)-ln(C_it); r_it = alpha_i + lambda_t + X_i,t-1 beta + u_it

Positive r means a reduction. beta is an association between a predetermined characteristic and the annual reduction within the observed eligible population.

Lagged variables and fixed effects do not resolve reverse causality. Intensity can improve through a growing denominator with unchanged physical energy or emissions. Broad solar-related imports do not establish solar installation.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.

- Primary noncausal FE association of adjacent-year reductions with lagged size/export/import/FDI, firm and year effects

- Adjacent pairs within 2010–2015 and 2017–2022;2015→2017 two-year difference audit separate
