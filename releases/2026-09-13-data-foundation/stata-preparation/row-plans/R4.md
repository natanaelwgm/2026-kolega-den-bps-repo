## Question

Which factories remained observable, closed, or recovered during COVID?

Keep actual closure distinct from survey disappearance, temporary gaps and movement outside the survey frame.

Unit: Fixed verified 2019 incumbent cohort with annual states 2020–2022; plant-year risk sets for confirmed closure.

### Target and meaning

h_it = Pr(closure_it=1 | alive_i,t-1=1, X_i,pre); Pr(state_i,2022=s | X_i,pre) = exp(X beta_s)/sum_r exp(X beta_r)

Hazard is a conditional closure risk only if closure is verified. Multinomial probabilities describe mutually exclusive 2022 trajectories of the 2019 cohort.

Observed absence is not business death. A model of post 2019 outcomes does not identify the causal effect of COVID. Right censoring and pre-existing frame changes matter.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.
