# R7 — FDI, greener technology and post-Paris foreign acquisition

**Planning only. This row is incomplete; no analysis was executed. All44 plans must pass the global freeze before execution.**

## Question

Does a verified foreign acquisition change a factory’s environmental performance, and do post-Paris acquisitions differ?

Distinguish a real ownership event from a recorded 10% foreign-capital threshold crossing, then compare cohorts at the same event horizon.

Unit: Verified continuing establishment-year with source-validated domestic-to-foreign acquisition events.

### Target and meaning

ATT_k^a = E[q_i,g+k(1)-q_i,g+k(0) | verified acquisition at g, cohort era=a]; Delta_k = ATT_k^post - ATT_k^pre

The first estimand concerns acquisition under the event-study assumptions. The post-minus-pre difference compares acquisition cohorts; it is not by itself the effect of Paris.

Targets of foreign acquisition are selected. A foreign-share threshold is not necessarily a transfer of control. Lower intensity with higher total energy is not proof of a particular green technology.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.

## Finite estimation family

Primary outcomes: ln(V/E_total); ln(V/C_total).

Secondary outcomes: ln(E_total); ln(C_total); ln(V); ln(Y).

Methods:

- Primary BJS plant/year fixed-effect imputation around independently verified acquisitions; retained reversals define an acquisition-entry path, not continuous foreign control. CS doubly robust is the named sensitivity.
- Pre/post cohort contrast on one common event horizon and weighting rule
- Threshold-crossing event sensitivity explicitly distinct from verified acquisition

Windows:

- Pre era: verified 2012 and 2013 cohorts with complete actual g−2,g−1 and k=0,1,2; 2014 has only an available-horizon diagnostic.
- Post era: verified 2019,2020 cohorts with the same pre/post calendar support
- 2015–2018 excluded from the common-horizon era contrast; 2016 is absent

Primary outcomes run under the registered primary method for each relevant exposure and supported declared calendar/horizon. Secondary outcomes run under that same primary method only. Each sensitivity changes exactly its named design decision and runs primary outcomes only, except explicitly registered component-identity checks. Legacy, switching-block, policy-specific, acquisition-era and accounting branches use their own compatible population/window; never take a blind Cartesian product.

Freeze model_id manifest before execution. Persist every intended cell including source_blocked, unsupported, non converged and omit ted terms. No outcome/covariate/threshold selection by significance.

Two productivity outcomes by two era summaries = four tests; two post-minus-pre summary contrasts form a separate two-test family. Every era summary uses one-third weight on each of k=0,1,2 and is unavailable if any is unsupported.
