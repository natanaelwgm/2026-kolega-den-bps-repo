Status: **upfront plan; no analytical execution; row not complete.**


## Pertanyaan

Pada pabrik yang sama, apakah turunnya emisi per nilai tambah berjalan bersama berkurangnya pekerja atau membaiknya kegiatan dan pekerjaan?

Compare workforce, compensation, value-added and capital changes across plants with rapid declines or rises in carbon intensity. Keep intermediate and verified zero-carbon endpoint cases visible. These are simultaneous observed associations, not effects of decarbonisation.

d_i=(ln(C_i1/VA_i1)−ln(C_i0/VA_i0))/(year1−year0); rapid_fall=d_i<ln(0.9); rise=d_i>0; ΔL=L1−L0
