## Rumus, simbol, dan makna


r_it=ln(VA_real_it)-beta_L*ln(L_it)-beta_K*ln(K_real_it)

- **Y**: Preferred real value added for the required branch; never generic gross output in this two-input equation

- **L**: labor input

- **K**: real capital stock

- **beta L/beta K**: method-specific estimated elasticities

- **omega**: estimated log revenue-productivity residual; a plant-specific price or measurement component may remain

- **r**: common four-method log value-added production residual, including transitory measurement/output error; not a direct measure of pure technical efficiency

## Method Protocol

### required production concept

Value-added Cobb–Douglas in real value added, workers and real capital stock. This is a modeling choice for the requested four-method comparison, conditional on a defensible value-added production specification.

### gross output extension

Not a substitute for the required branch. A separately registered future extension adds beta_M*ln(M_real) to the structural production function and must pass its own flexible-input identification/moment audit before execution.

### technology cells

Verified harmonized two-digit manufacturing industries, pooled over actual supplied calendar years; no ad hoc industry pooling to force convergence.

### minimum support

Operational screen: at least 50 distinct real plants, 250 eligible dynamic records and six observed calendar waves per technology-method cell, plus full required design/instrument rank. These thresholds are practical precision screens, not a proof of identification.

### controls

Primary: no extra control() variables, within verified two-digit technology pools; this maintains an explicit common proxy/Markov specification across supplied years. Calendar composition is displayed. A year-control extension is not in the required execution grid and requires a separately frozen timing/normalization specification before fitting.

### polynomial

B_d(k,p) contains all monomials k^a p^b with 1<=a+b<=d; an intercept is included by regression. d=3 primary, d=2 sensitivity. ACF uses all monomials in l,k,p of total degree 1..d. Do not duplicate linear labor columns. The installed OP/LP/ACF code uses cubic lagged productivity even when poly(2) is selected. The degree-2 sensitivity therefore changes the first-stage proxy polynomial only; no claim that all nuisance stages become quadratic.

### timing

Exact method-specific equations, instruments and timing are enumerated in pinned_method_contract. No additional instruments or controls are selected during fitting.

### calendar

Lags are exact t-1 or t-2 calendar lags; do not treat 2015 as the one-year lag of 2017. No conditioning on future long-lived plants to select the main sample.

### OP selection

Required selection probability is estimated from independently authenticated risk/exit data, using the declared lagged B_d(k,p_OP) on the observed risk set. Selection outcome must not be inferred from the last row of the estimator-filtered sample. Unknown closure/risk status is excluded with a reason. ### source audit finding

Installed prodest derives exit as the final observed row before maxDate, silently drops attrition correction on selection-model failure, randomizes default starts, and counts the original fit inside reps. These behaviors are incompatible with blindly launching the previous generic G1 plan.

### required command options

All four commands explicitly specify valueadded, free(log_workers), state(log_real_capital), the method-specific proxy, real id() and actual t(). Assert e(model)==valueadded. Use method(op), method(lp), method(lp) acf, and method(wrdg) gmm respectively. Never rely on the package gross-output default. The required OP selection branch explicitly uses attrition; the adapter forces the selection path and asserts a successful authenticated selection receipt. Failure cannot fall through to an uncorrected fit.
