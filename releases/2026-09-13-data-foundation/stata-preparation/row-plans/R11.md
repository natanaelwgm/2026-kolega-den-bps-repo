## Question

Are improvements in environmental performance associated with more jobs, higher pay, or a different workforce?

Describe within-factory associations while separating shared numerators, output expansion and workforce composition from technological or causal claims.

Unit: Verified establishment-year and true adjacent-year change pairs; workforce components reconciled to annual totals.

### Target and meaning

Delta ln(L_it) = lambda_t + beta Delta ln(V_it/E_it) + u_it; FE: ln(L_it)=alpha_i+lambda_t+beta ln(V_it/E_it)+u_it

The coefficient describes a change or within-factory association. Carbon productivity replaces energy productivity in a separate model; underlying V, E, C, Y, L components are always reported.

Jobs, output and energy can respond simultaneously to demand. A common value-added numerator can mechanically relate productivity ratios. Wage per worker mixes pay and composition; absent wages cannot be zeros. A causal policy branch requires a new credible design.

### Formula symbols

- **i**: a verified establishment, never a row-number or artificial linkage.
- **t**: actual calendar year.
- **V**: value added deflated with a certified appropriate price index; any single-output-deflator approximation is named.
- **Y**: gross output deflated with its certified output-price index.
- **E**: scope-defined verified physical energy in GJ; incomplete observed subtotals are separately named.
- **C**: scope-defined verified CO2 in metric tonnes; not automatically all greenhouse gases.
- **L**: workers under an annually verified employment definition.
- **X**: predetermined measured covariates declared for the particular design.
- **g**: verified treatment-entry cohort in calendar years.
- **k**: calendar event time t-g.
- **S**: a verified trade, ownership, industry, or survival state; unknown is retained as unknown.
